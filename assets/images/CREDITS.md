# Photo credits and reuse rights

The six additional photographs below are U.S. government works. Their source pages identify the authors and reuse status. They are separate from the repository's software license. Source and rights pages were checked on 2026-10-26.

The website links each photograph's NPS or NASA credit to its source. Photos are shared at `/assets/images/` on both pages. Local JPEGs were resized and compressed, and WebP previews were generated for faster loading. The centered DSCOVR Earth image was converted from NASA’s PNG to lossless WebP; decoded pixels were verified identical.

NPS source pages identify the photographs as employee works produced in their official duties and public domain in the United States. See the [NPS copyright policy](https://www.nps.gov/aboutus/disclaimer.htm).
